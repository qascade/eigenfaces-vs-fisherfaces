=== E3: glasses vs no glasses (ORL) ===
   117 of 400 images wear glasses (29.2%)
   on the UNbalanced set, always answering "no" would score 70.8%,
   which beats any real method -- hence the balancing below.
   balanced subset: 234 images, 50.0% with glasses

   method            our error (mean of 10 draws)    paper (Yale, 36 images)
   PCA, k = 10           43.1%  (37.2 to 48.3)        52.6%
   one Fisherface        41.5%  (35.9 to 50.9)         5.3%
   always say "no"       50.0%                     50.0%
   Neither method works here, and the spread over draws is wide.
   We reproduce that PCA is near chance, but NOT that Fisherfaces
   solve it.  Their 36 images were PAIRED (the same person with and
   without glasses, one session); in ORL glasses come with changes
   of pose, expression and session, so there is no clean signal.
